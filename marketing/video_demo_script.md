# 30-Second Video Demonstration Script

**Format**: 9:16 Vertical Video (TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Reddit / X)
**Props**: Galaxy Z Flip (Folded, resting on desk or held in hand)

---

### [00:00 - 00:06] The Hook
- **Visual**: Show folded Galaxy Z Flip resting on a table. Cover screen is black/asleep.
- **Voiceover**: *"If you have a Galaxy Z Flip and use Home Assistant, this is the fastest way to control your home."*

### [00:06 - 00:14] The Action (Zero-Latency Tap)
- **Visual**: Double tap the cover screen to wake the clock. Point finger at the bottom right pill overlay. Tap the "Building Door" or "Living Room" button.
- **Visual Effect**: Button pulses green with instant haptic pop. Show light turning on or door opening in background.
- **Voiceover**: *"No swiping through Good Lock widgets, no waiting for WebViews to load. Tap and it fires in 100 milliseconds — even while the phone is locked."*

### [00:14 - 00:22] Touch Passthrough Demo
- **Visual**: Swipe across the clock and native humidity/weather widgets. Show that Samsung widgets still respond 100% smoothly.
- **Voiceover**: *"And your native clock and widgets work completely untouched because touches pass straight through."*

### [00:22 - 00:30] Call to Action
- **Visual**: Show the Material 3 configuration dashboard on the unfolded screen with the button editor.
- **Voiceover**: *"Fully open source, encrypted, and free on GitHub. Link in the bio/comments!"*
