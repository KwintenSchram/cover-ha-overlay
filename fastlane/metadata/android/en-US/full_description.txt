Cover HA Overlay is a lightweight, privacy-first open-source utility designed specifically for Samsung Galaxy Z Flip (Flip5, Flip6, Flip7) foldable devices.

It places an always-accessible, floating quick-control bar directly on your outer cover screen (Flex Window). Control lights, door locks, switches, curtains, and automations without opening your phone or waiting for full-screen apps to load.

Key Features:
- Instant Zero-Latency Actions: Background REST API dispatch in ~100ms with zero Activity popups.
- Locked Cover Screen Support: Trigger smart home actions directly from your locked cover screen.
- True Touch Passthrough: Fully interacts with native Samsung widgets and clock without interception.
- Smart Safety Guards: Conditional execution based on Home Assistant binary sensors and double-tap confirmation for door locks.
- End-to-End Local Security: Hardware-backed Android Keystore AES-256-GCM encryption with zero cloud intermediaries.
- Zero Idle Drain: Automatically disconnects WebSockets when the cover display sleeps for 0% CPU consumption.

Permissions:
- Draw Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Required to render the floating quick-control bar on the secondary display.
- Unrestricted Battery: Required to prevent One UI from killing the background service.
